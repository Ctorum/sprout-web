# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Individuals and couples managing household money, plus small-business owners who switch to a company context. They open Sprout at home in the evening or on their phone during the day for short, routine sessions, mostly to check whether goals and debts are moving as planned.

## Product Purpose
A single place to manage personal, shared and business finances: credits, debts, investments and goals. The name signals growth built steadily. Success is the user seeing progress toward what they want (a house, a trip, a debt cleared) and feeling in control.

## Brand Personality
Bold, modern, agile. Confident and fast, with strong visual character, but never hype-driven: energy comes from clarity and motion of data, not celebration.

## Anti-references
- Gamified fintech: confetti, streaks, emojis, rewards
- Cold, legacy-bank grey dashboards
- Generic SaaS dashboard: big-number hero tiles and identical card grids

## Design Principles
1. Goals lead: every screen answers "am I on track?" before "what do I have?".
2. One system, three scales: solo, couple and company share one model and layout; the context switcher changes data, not look.
3. Energy without noise: bold color and contrast carry personality; density stays calm and scannable.
4. Honest numbers: debt and shortfalls are shown plainly, not alarming, not hidden.
5. Growth is earned: progress appears in how data evolves over time, not in decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA in both light and dark themes. Never rely on color alone for gain/loss or debt/credit (always sign, icon or label). Respect reduced motion. Keyboard-complete, screen-reader-friendly tables and charts. Currency and locale-aware formatting (pt and en).
