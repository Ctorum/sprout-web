# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People managing money at three scales: an individual, a couple or household, and a small company. They open Sprout to check where they stand, record or review credits, debts and investments, and track progress toward goals. Sessions are routine, often repeated, and sometimes stressful (debt, shared decisions).

## Product Purpose
A single place to manage personal, shared and business finances: credits, debts, investments, objectives and more. The name signals growth and nurturing: financial health built steadily over time. Success is the user feeling in control and seeing progress, not just seeing numbers.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, trustworthy. Patient and clear, never hype-driven. Growth is shown through steady progress, not celebration.

## Anti-references
- Crypto/trading-app neon and urgency
- Gamified, confetti-heavy fintech
- Cold, legacy-bank grey dashboards
- Generic SaaS dashboard: big-number hero tiles and identical card grids
- Literal plant clichés (leaf logos everywhere, green-on-white by reflex)

## Design Principles
1. Clarity over cleverness: every number is legible, labeled and trustworthy at a glance.
2. Progress, not pressure: show trajectory over time; debt and shortfalls get honest, non-alarming treatment.
3. One system, three scales: personal, couple and company views share one model and change context, not look.
4. Calm density: enough information to decide, with quiet hierarchy.
5. Growth is earned: the metaphor shows up in how data evolves, not in decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Never rely on color alone for gain/loss or debt/credit. Respect reduced motion. Keyboard-complete, screen-reader-friendly tables and charts. Currency and locale-aware number formatting.
